Rules

   customer_id (from id), name (from full_name), email (from email), country (from country), tier (from plan).

   a. status equals "active" (exact, case-sensitive)

   Filters run before the dedup, so a row removed by a filter never counts as a duplicate.
4. Output rows keep the input order of the kept rows. Header row first. UTF-8, LF, RFC 4180 quoting (a field with a comma or a quote is quoted).
